# Data

Cable and network data accompanying the companion paper, released so the
verification can be reproduced end to end.

**Provenance and licensing.** The repository's MIT license covers the code.
The linecode, impedance, and other data generated by the authors are released
under CC BY 4.0; see [`data/LICENSE`](LICENSE). The feeder
topology under `network_1/Feeder_1/` derives from the ENWL Low Voltage
Network Solutions dataset (Electricity North West Ltd. and The University
of Manchester, Low Carbon Networks Fund), published for use by the research
and engineering communities and subsequently adapted as the IEEE European
LV Test Feeder; it is redistributed here, with attribution, for research
reproducibility.

## `network_1/Feeder_1/`

ENWL LV test Network 1, Feeder 1 (OpenDSS), the deployment network of the
paper's case studies: a three-phase four-wire trunk plus single-phase
laterals serving 55 loads. `standarized_linecodes.dss` carries the
finite-element linecodes (LineCableModels.jl, Darwin impedance +
electrodynamic admittance; earth ρ = 100 Ωm, f = 50 Hz) for the
NAYCWY 4×95 trunk and the NAYCY 1×16 lateral. Original feeder data
published by Navarro-Espinosa et al. (ENWL LV Network Solutions), later
released as the IEEE European LV Test Feeder.

## `zprim/`

Primitive matrices (JLD2) for the cables under study, one file per
(cable, formulation): the series impedance `Z_primitive` (Ω/km) and, where
present, the shunt admittance `Y_primitive` (S/m), with the conductors ordered
as phases a, b, c, then the neutral.

- Cables: `NAYCWY_3x95` (explicit concentric stranded neutral),
  `NAYY_3x95` (three-wire reduction), `NAYY_4x95`.
- Formulations: `fem` (finite-element reference, ONELAB/Darwin),
  `full_carson`, `simple_carson` (analytical), `deri`.
- `NAYY_4x95_rho_e_*` variants sweep the earth resistivity
  (10, 30, 100, 300 Ωm); they carry the series impedance only.

Shunt admittance:

- `fem`: electrodynamic finite-element solution of the same geometry.
- Analytical NAYCWY 3×95: Maxwell potential coefficients of the three sector
  cores inside the grounded concentric neutral (each core as the equal-area
  circle at its centroid, image method, PVC permittivity), plus the
  neutral-to-earth capacitance through the PVC jacket. Core self capacitance
  0.98 nF/m (FE: 0.72 nF/m).
- Analytical NAYY designs: no analytical shunt (`Y_primitive` = 0). The FE
  files carry it; at LV and power frequency it has no effect on the
  verification verdict (shunt-omitted case of the paper).

## `kron_case/`

Archived outputs of the paper's n = 30 Kron-reduction run (Table 1,
case 1), consumed by `examples/kron_reduction_case.jl`:

- `kron_dj_n30.csv` — per-realisation optimal DSSE objectives
  J*(M_r), J*(M_c) and the measurement count |M| for the FE
  explicit-neutral reference against its Kron-reduced candidate.
- `block3_en_vs_krn.csv` — pooled alarm confusion counts (VV, NEV,
  VUF, PVUR) and the MAPE_U diagnostic of the same run.
